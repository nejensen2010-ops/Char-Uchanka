Abilities and weapons

Core Abilities

  1. Claws + modulation of claw sharpness

    uchanka can use her will to sharpen her claws across 3 distinct modes while also being able to do every inbetween:

    • dull: regular human nail sharpness
    • precision thread: a medium edge that is too dull to cut threads easily but is still sharp enough to cut.
    • maximum sharpness: sharper than a 0-degree bevel, and is dense enough, usually, to deflect nichirin blades in close combat

  2. Regeneration:
    In her base states, this can heal and regenerate her entire hand in approximately 1 second or so, in her awakened state that time drops to match nezuko's awakened state regeneration which is Upper-Moon Rank regeneration
  3. Total Concentration Breathing:
    she learned this from Kyojuro Rengoku, she has no particular breathing style, but can use this without having to think about it at all.

Blood Demon Art

Uchanka's blood demon art works through energetic manupulation to make constructs.

when she forms a construct, it leaves a thread connected to a part of her body, usually her hands unless she moves it to be attached to other areas. she learned how to reattach it to other areas during the rehab training after mount natagumo. this rehab training was held by kurojiro rengoku.

when she uses her constructs her body tends to glow extremely faintly. usually this color is primarily blue, tinted with a crimson blood red color. however this crimson red color can shift with her emotional state, follows the same color theory that her eyes do in her demonic states.
